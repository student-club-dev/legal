Student Club — Child Safety Standards

App: Student Club (Android package uz.studentclub.app) · Developer: Topshiriq.uz · Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. Zero tolerance for CSAE

Student Club strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • creating, uploading, sharing or requesting child sexual abuse material (CSAM);
  • grooming, sexualising or soliciting minors, or any sexual contact with a minor;
  • sextortion, trafficking, or any content or conduct that endangers children.

This applies to profiles, photos, listings, stories, posts, groups, clubs and private messages.

2. Enforcement

Content that violates these standards is removed as soon as we become aware of it, and the responsible accounts are permanently banned. We preserve relevant information as required by law and report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the competent law-enforcement authorities of the Republic of Uzbekistan and other relevant jurisdictions.

3. How to report

  • In the app: open the chat with that user and use Report; you can also Block the user at any time.
  • By email: write to our child safety contact below. Reports are reviewed with priority.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first (in Uzbekistan: 102).

4. Compliance with law

Student Club complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations, including the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan and Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.
