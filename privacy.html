Student Club — Privacy Policy

App: Student Club (Android package uz.studentclub.app) · Developer: Topshiriq.uz · Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Student Club mobile app collects, why, and what control you have over it.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: first and last name, phone number, university, and (optionally) a university email address.
  • Profile photo, if you add one.
  • Content you create: listings, stories, posts, group and chat messages, and media you send.
  • Technical data: a push notification token and basic device information needed to deliver notifications and keep the service working.

2. Why we use it

To identify your account, connect you with students from your university, show listings, deliver messages and notifications, and keep the app safe (including handling reports of abuse). We do not sell your data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Who can see it

Your name, photo, university and listings are visible to other students in the app. Your phone number is shown only when you put it in a listing yourself. Private messages are visible only to the participants of that chat.

4. Permissions

Camera, microphone, photo gallery and location are requested only when you start the related action (sending a photo, recording a voice message, showing distance to a discount). If you decline, the rest of the app keeps working.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — to deliver push notifications.
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose to sign in with Google.

6. How long we keep it

Stories disappear from the feed after 24 hours and remain in your private archive; their files are deleted after 365 days. Other data is kept until you delete it or delete your account.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and passwords are stored only in a non-reversible (hashed) form. No system is 100% secure, so please use a strong password.

8. Your rights and account deletion

You can edit your profile, delete your listings and stories, or delete your account at any time in Settings. Deleting the account deletes your personal data from our servers.

9. Children

Student Club is intended for university students and is not directed at children. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation — see our Child Safety Standards.

Student Club — Maxfiylik siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 22-oktabr

1. Qanday ma'lumot yig'amiz

Ism-familya, telefon raqam, universitet va (ixtiyoriy) universitet emaili, profil rasmi. Shuningdek siz qo'ygan e'lonlar, hikoyalar, postlar, guruh va chat xabarlari hamda yuborgan media saqlanadi. Bildirishnomalarni yetkazish uchun push token va qurilma haqida asosiy texnik ma'lumot ishlatiladi.

2. Nima uchun kerak

Hisobingizni tanish, sizni universitetdoshlaringiz bilan bog'lash, e'lonlarni ko'rsatish, xabar va bildirishnomalarni yetkazish hamda xavfsizlikni ta'minlash (shikoyatlarni ko'rib chiqish) uchun. Ma'lumotlar sotilmaydi va uchinchi tomon reklamasi uchun ishlatilmaydi.

3. Kim ko'radi

Ism, rasm, universitet va e'lonlaringiz boshqa talabalarga ko'rinadi. Telefon raqamingiz faqat siz uni e'londa ko'rsatgan holda ochiladi. Shaxsiy yozishmalar faqat suhbat ishtirokchilariga ko'rinadi.

4. Ruxsatlar

Kamera, mikrofon, galereya va joylashuv faqat siz o'sha amalni boshlaganda so'raladi. Ruxsat bermasangiz ilovaning qolgan qismi ishlashda davom etadi.

5. Uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push bildirishnomalar) va Google orqali kirish (faqat siz tanlasangiz).

6. Qancha saqlanadi

Hikoyalar 24 soatdan keyin lentadan yo'qoladi va arxivingizda qoladi; ularning fayllari 365 kundan keyin o'chiriladi. Qolgan ma'lumotlar siz o'chirguningizcha yoki hisob o'chirilgunicha saqlanadi.

7. Xavfsizlik

Ma'lumotlar shifrlangan kanal (HTTPS) orqali uzatiladi, parollar qaytarib bo'lmaydigan ko'rinishda saqlanadi.

8. Huquqlaringiz va hisobni o'chirish

Profilni tahrirlash, e'lon va hikoyalarni o'chirish yoki hisobni butunlay o'chirish Sozlamalar orqali istalgan vaqtda mumkin. Hisob o'chirilganda shaxsiy ma'lumotlar serverlarimizdan o'chiriladi.

9. Bolalar

Student Club universitet talabalari uchun mo'ljallangan va bolalarga qaratilmagan. Bolalarga nisbatan jinsiy zo'ravonlik va ekspluatatsiyaga mutlaqo yo'l qo'yilmaydi — Bolalar xavfsizligi standartlariga qarang.
